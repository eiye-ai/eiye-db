01

Who this is for

Teams about to put an agent in front of customers, employees, or a client demo — especially if the agent uses tools, retrieval, or multiple steps, and they need someone who did not build it to say whether it is ready.

02

What we do

Two weeks. Independent of the people who built the agent.

  1. 01
    Agree what “good” means

    Jobs the agent should do, and jobs it must not do.

  2. 02
    Run held-out tasks

    Including cases the team may not have tested.

  3. 03
    Probe the obvious failure modes

    Skipped steps, bad tool use, made-up answers, policy or permission slip-throughs, and cases that look improved but still are not correct.

  4. 04
    Stay independent of the builders

    The review is not done by the people who built the agent.

03

What you get. What this is not.

A written verdict with evidence. Not a build, and not a piece of software.

You get

  • A written verdict: what held, what failed, and the severity
  • Evidence for each finding: inputs, outputs, and what was expected
  • A short ranked list of fixes before shipping
  • A walkthrough

This is not

  • A build, a rewrite, or a monitoring subscription
  • A downloadable plugin, or a package you can npm-install
  • eiye_db, and not a new software tier
  • Implementing the fixes, or standing the agent up — that is a separate engagement

  • A short description of intended use
